# Aurora inside Iterali — positioning brief

## Date
2026-07-20

## Positioning

**Primary:** Aurora – an Iterali story world, where workplace decisions shape what survives.

**Short alt:** Practise real decisions under pressure, inside a story that keeps you in the moment.

**Team line:** Aurora for engagement. Iterali for skill patterns. Same safe-to-fail practice.

## Description

Aurora is Iterali’s narrative layer: a living investigation world where something is already going wrong and every choice has weight. You are not ticking modules. You are inside a case, reading messages, weighing shortcuts and deciding while the clock and the story push back.

Those choices mirror work. Urgency vs checking. Trust vs verify. Speed vs process. A risky click in Aurora stands for the same skill as a phishing mail at busy moments. Feedback stays human: what cue you used, what risk it maps to, what safer step looks like. Mistakes are part of the story, not a mark against you.

For teams, Aurora sessions feed Iterali’s promise: aggregated skill patterns and gaps, not individual mistake replays. Aurora pulls people in. Iterali turns practice into confidence in everyday decisions, with minimal data and clear explanations.

## Site CTA

Enter Aurora, Iterali’s story world. Practise the decisions you face at work, review without blame and build team skill patterns without checkbox theatre or individual mistake replays.
